Frequently asked questions

Is CRYPTO LAB a cryptocurrency exchange?

This starter website is an information and education platform. Exchange, custody, deposits, withdrawals, or investment features should only be added with appropriate legal, security, and compliance requirements.

Are the market prices live?

No. The figures on this demo are clearly marked as illustrative placeholders. A production site should connect to a reputable market-data provider before displaying live prices.

Can customer support ask for my seed phrase?

No. Legitimate support should never request your seed phrase, private key, password, or one-time authentication code.
